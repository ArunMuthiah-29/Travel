    Trusted Travel Company

    Founded with a strong vision by Arun M, our travel company was created to deliver reliable, affordable, and memorable travel experiences. What began as a passionate idea has grown into a trusted travel agency known for quality service and customer satisfaction. Under the leadership of Founder Arun M, we specialize in customized tour packages, honeymoon trips, group tours, and family vacations across India and international destinations. Every journey is carefully planned to ensure comfort, safety, and unforgettable memories. With years of experience in the travel industry, we continue to evolve by embracing innovation while staying committed to our core values of trust, transparency, and personalized service. Today, we proudly stand as a dependable travel partner, helping travelers explore the world with confidence.

    Why AS Travels?

    AS Travels was founded with a clear purpose — to make every journey smooth, safe, and memorable. Led by Founder Arun M, we focus on delivering reliable travel services with complete transparency and customer satisfaction.
